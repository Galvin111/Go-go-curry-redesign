KANAZAWA-STYLE CURRY

What Makes Kanazawa Curry Unique?

Unlike light yellow or watery curry varieties, authentic Kanazawa Curry is famous across Japan for its ultra-rich, dark mahogany brown appearance, caramelized aroma, and velvety gravy texture that thoroughly coats every grain of rice.

Traditionally served on a classic stainless steel plate with a fork or spork, Kanazawa curry is always accompanied by crisp, thinly shredded fresh cabbage to balance the intense umami profile of the sauce.

The 55-Step Culinary Process

The name "Go Go" stands for the number 55 in Japanese ("Go" = 5). Our master recipe is governed by strict culinary precision:

05

5 Hours of Simmering

Premium herbs, spices, and braised ingredients are slowly caramelized in giant cauldrons over open flames for 5 hours.

55

55 Hours of Resting

The curry base is allowed to rest and mature for 55 hours, deepening the flavor complex into velvet perfection.

55

55 Steps of Quality

From rice rinsing to fresh cabbage cutting and golden panko frying, 55 meticulous quality checks govern every dish.

THE GORILLA MASCOT

Why The Gorilla?

In Japan, the gorilla symbolizes unmatched strength, power, and unstoppable energy.

Our founder was inspired by Kanazawa's hometown baseball legend Hideki Matsui (whose nickname was "Godzilla" / #55). Combining the number 55 with the fierce strength of the gorilla, Go Go Curry was born in May 2004!
