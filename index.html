THE 55-STEP SECRET

What Makes Go Go Curry So Addictive?

Originating in Kanazawa, Ishikawa Prefecture, Go Go Curry brings authentic Japanese curry culture to America. Our signature curry sauce is simmered for 5 hours and rested for 55 hours to unleash deep caramelization and velvety flavor profiles.

  • ✔ Served over premium Japanese short-grain rice
  • ✔ Topped with crisp, hand-breaded pork or chicken katsu
  • ✔ Accompanied by freshly shredded cabbage for crunch
